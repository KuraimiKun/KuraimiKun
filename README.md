### 👨‍💻 About Me

I design and build **multi-tenant SaaS platforms**, **high-throughput bot & messaging infrastructures**, **applied AI/ML pipelines**, and **cross-platform mobile applications**. My open-source work spans enterprise resource planning (ERP), quantitative trading & blockchain fraud detection, realtime WebRTC communications, and automated digital forensics.

- 🏗️ **Enterprise & Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Per-tenant PostgreSQL database isolation, distributed task queues (Celery/Redis), Clean Architecture (.NET 8), and microservices.
- 🤖 **Multi-Bot & Realtime Systems:** Shared-core Telegram & WhatsApp bot platforms handling payments, media pipelines, and WebRTC signaling.
- 🧠 **AI, Graph Learning & Security:** Temporal Graph Attention Networks (PyTorch Geometric) for multi-chain fraud detection, Explainable AI (SHAP/LIME), and automated PDF tampering forensics.
